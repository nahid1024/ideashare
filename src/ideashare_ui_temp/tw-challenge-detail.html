About this challenge

Dhaka University wants to hear from students, educators, employers, and everyday people. Traditional admissions systems rely heavily on exam scores — but is that the best way to find great students?

Submit your boldest idea for how university admissions should work. Think about fairness, access, skill recognition, and what universities are really trying to find in a student.

💡
The best ideas will be shared directly with Dhaka University's admissions board. The top 3 ideas also win cash prizes.
